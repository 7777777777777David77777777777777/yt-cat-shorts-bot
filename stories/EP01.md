# EP01

## 標題

Little Girl Brings Stray Kitten Home to Her 90-Pound Husky

## 氛圍

Warm friendship with gentle humor

## 標籤

cute cat, little girl and kitten, husky, wholesome story, 3d animation, cute animals, shorts

## 角色

- Mia, a cute 6-year-old girl with brown pigtails wearing a yellow raincoat
- Mochi, a tiny fluffy orange tabby kitten with big green eyes
- Titan, a giant friendly silver-and-white Siberian Husky dog

## 第1幕

### 旁白

Six-year-old Mia found a tiny shivering ginger kitten in the rain and brought him home to meet her giant Husky, Titan.

### 畫面

Cute 6-year-old girl Mia in a yellow raincoat holding a tiny orange tabby kitten in a hallway, introducing the kitten to a giant silver-and-white Siberian Husky dog

## 第2幕

### 旁白

Everyone worried the ninety-pound dog would scare the kitten, but Titan gently laid down and offered his warm furry paw.

### 畫面

The giant silver-and-white Siberian Husky dog lying flat on the floor offering his big white front paw to the tiny orange tabby kitten, while the 6-year-old girl watches happily

## 第3幕

### 旁白

Within minutes, little Mochi was fast asleep on Titan's chest while Mia covered them both with her favorite soft blanket.

### 畫面

The tiny orange tabby kitten sleeping peacefully on top of the giant Siberian Husky dog's chest by a fireplace, while the 6-year-old girl places a soft blanket over the dog and cat
