womenswear, outfit try on, knit top, straight leg pants, crew socks, fashion, shorts

## 角色

- Ava, a 28-year-old adult woman fashion model with dark brown hair in a low ponytail, brown eyes and natural makeup. She wears the same outfit in every scene: a cream long-sleeve crewneck knit top, high-waisted charcoal straight-leg ankle-length trousers, ivory ribbed crew socks and clean white low-top sneakers. Keep her face, hairstyle, garment colors, fabric textures and outfit proportions consistent.
